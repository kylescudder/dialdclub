Diald Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Diald app ("Diald", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using Diald you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic profile details those services share (such as your name and email).
  • Content you create. Brew data you enter in the app — beans, extraction times, ratios, brew methods, grind, temperature, tasting notes, reminders, and any photos you choose to add.
  • Device tokens. If you enable notifications, we store a push notification token so we can deliver brew reminders.
  • Diagnostics. We collect crash reports and basic diagnostic data to keep the app stable.

How we use your information

  • To provide and sync your account and brew data across your devices.
  • To send reminders and notifications you have enabled.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix problems.

How your information is stored

Your account and brew data are stored using Supabase, our backend and database provider. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected with row-level security so that you can only access your own records.

Third-party services

We rely on the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage.
  • Sentry — crash reporting and diagnostics.
  • Apple Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In — optional account sign-in.
  • Apple Push Notification service — delivery of notifications.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete content or your account, the relevant records are removed from active use and permanently deleted in due course. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children

Diald is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be communicated within the app.

Contact us
